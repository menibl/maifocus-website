How it works

From a flood of alerts to a single clear picture

A night in the control room, with MAI beside the operator.

01
● Alert

An alert hits the screen. MAI reacts instantly.

The moment something happens, MAI notices, turns to it and starts working. You don't have to hunt for the event among dozens of feeds.

02
● Analysing

It checks every camera and filters the noise.

MAI moves from monitor to monitor, verifies each alert, and fades out the false ones. Only the events that are real stay highlighted.

03
● Acting

It works inside the software you already use.

MAI opens the camera management window, selects the right camera, takes a screenshot and reports to the operator, exactly as a trained team member would.

04
● Insight

Then it explains what it found.

Patterns, peaks and anomalies are summarised on a live dashboard, so tomorrow's shift starts smarter than today's.
